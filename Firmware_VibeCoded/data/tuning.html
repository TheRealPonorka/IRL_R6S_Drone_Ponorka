🆘 Troubleshooting Quick Reference:

  • 🔴 Violent oscillation: Reduce Kp, increase Kd
  • 🟡 No response to tilt: Increase Kp, check motor connections
  • 🟡 Slow drift: Add small amount of Ki
  • 🟠 Unstable at speed: Tune Velocity PID in code
  • ⚫ Motors don't spin: Check power, connections, emergency stop
